Light Wrap

Let the background's light bleed onto the edges of a character, so it sits in the scene instead of looking pasted on. Add Light Wrap from the Light category, connect the character to Source and the background to Background.

Output: Over Background composites the wrapped character over the background, so the background needs no other connection. Foreground Only outputs the character alone, with the light on its edges, for compositing elsewhere.

Wrap Width is how far the light reaches into the character from its edge, fading to nothing; Softness blurs the background first, so the light is its overall colour rather than its details; both are in pixels at 100%. Intensity scales the light. Blend is Screen, which brightens without exceeding white, or Add, which is stronger. The character's alpha does not change.

Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported, and the result does not depend on render tiles.
