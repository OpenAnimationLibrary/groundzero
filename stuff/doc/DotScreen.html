Dot Screen

Draws a lattice of dots: a halftone of an image, a decorative pattern, or stripes. Add Dot Screen from the Render category. With nothing connected it draws a plain pattern. Connect an image to Source and it becomes a halftone of that image: dark areas get large dots, light areas small ones and transparent areas none.

Lattice

Spacing is the distance between dot centres and Angle turns the lattice about the centre of the scene. Row Stagger shifts every other row by a share of a cell; 0.5 gives a brick pattern. Jitter lets each dot leave its place by up to that share of a cell.

Dot

Shape is Round, Square, Diamond or Line. A line is one cell long: lines join into stripes along their rows, or along the columns when turned 90 degrees. Size is the dot's height as a share of the spacing: at 1 a round dot touches its neighbours. Aspect is width over height, Rotation turns each dot on top of the lattice angle, and Edge Softness blurs the dot's edge. Each dot takes a colour between Colour and Second Colour, as far as Colour Randomness allows. The randomness amounts shrink, turn and fade dots at random; Hold redraws the random values every that many frames, and Seed picks them.

Modifiers

Each of the three modifiers reads one input at the centre of every dot: Source, Control2 or Control3. Changes picks what it drives: Size, Aspect, Rotation, Opacity or Colour; Off ignores the input. The input's brightness picks a value between At Black and At White, and By says what that value does: Set replaces the dot's value, Multiply scales it, Add adds to it. A rotation to Set or Add is in degrees; Multiply takes a plain factor, so 2 doubles the rotation. Invert swaps black and white. For Colour, each dot takes the input's colour at its centre, as far as At White allows (1 takes it fully).

Transparent areas of a modifier's input get no dots, whatever the modifier changes, so a halftone of a drawing on a transparent level stays inside the drawing. Where the input is partly transparent the dots fade with it; its brightness there is that of its own colour, not darkened by the transparency.

The first modifier starts as Size, Multiply, 1 at black and 0 at white, which is a halftone of Source.

Presets

The FX's presets hold Halftone, Diagonal Lines, Mosaic (which needs the image on Control2) and Polka Dots.

Starting points

Halftone: connect the image to Source and leave the defaults; lower Spacing for a finer screen. Coloured dots: connect the same image to Control2 and set Modifier 2 to Colour. Diagonal stripes: Shape Line, Angle 45, Size 0.5. Mosaic: Shape Square, Angle 0, Size 1, Modifier 1 Off and Modifier 2 on Colour.

Notes

Every value except Invert can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point renders are supported. The dots are tied to the scene, not to the render tiles or the preview size. The dots are drawn turned and scaled with the camera; an uneven stretch or a shear is applied to the finished screen.
