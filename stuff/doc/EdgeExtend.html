Edge Extend

Push the colour of a drawing's solid interior out over its antialiased edge, so later keys, blurs and colour corrections do not pick up the edge's mix with an old background (a dark fringe, a halo). Add Edge Extend from the Matte category and connect the image to Source.

Radius is how far from the interior colours are replaced, in pixels at 100%. Pixels at least as opaque as Solid From Alpha are interior and keep their colour; the others within the radius take the colour of the nearest interior pixel, at their own alpha. With Extend Alpha on, the band around the drawing becomes opaque too, for effects that need colour beyond the edge.

Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported, and the result does not depend on render tiles.
