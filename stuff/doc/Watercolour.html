Watercolour

Turns flat-colour artwork into a painted wash. Add Watercolour from the Stylize category and connect the drawing to Source. It works best on flat colours without lines; put line art over the result.

Wash

Edge Wobble is how far the edge of each colour wanders, in the drawing's own pixels, and Wobble Size is the size of the wander's features: small values make a ragged edge, large values a slow curve. Bleed is how far colour spreads past an edge, so neighbouring colours run into each other and into the paper.

Pigment

Edge Darkening gathers pigment at the edge of every colour, as a drying wash does: it is the share of the colour lost at the edge, and Edge Width is how far in from the edge it reaches. Edge Tolerance is how different two neighbouring pixels must be to count as an edge; raise it if a gradient or noise in the source is being outlined. Granulation makes the colour uneven inside each area, in grains of Granule Size.

Paper

With Paper on, the wash lies on an opaque sheet of Paper Colour that covers the drawing and the bleed around it; to fill the whole frame, put a Colour Card of the same colour under the result. With it off, the result keeps its transparency and can be laid over other artwork. Paper Grain darkens the result in a fine texture of Paper Grain Size. The grain belongs to the drawing: it moves and scales with it, and stays the same on every frame.

Timing

Hold is the number of frames each wash is kept before the wobble and the granulation change: 2 paints on twos, and a large value keeps one wash for a whole shot. Seed picks the sequence: the same seed always gives the same wash, so renders are repeatable.

Presets

The FX's presets hold Wet in Wet, Dry Brush and Transparent Wash; the defaults are a soft wash on paper.

Starting points

Soft wash: the defaults. Wet-in-wet: Bleed 12 or more, Edge Darkening 0. Dry brush: Bleed 1, Wobble Size 4, Granulation 0.6. Over line art: Paper off, and the line art above the result.

Notes

Every value except Paper can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported. Distances are in the drawing's own pixels, up to 64. The wash is tied to the drawing's position, not to the render tiles or the preview size, and the frames of one hold share a cache entry. With every amount at 0 and Paper off the image passes through exactly.
