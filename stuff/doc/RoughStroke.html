Rough Stroke

Redraws the strokes of a vector drawing as rough, hand-drawn strokes. Add Rough Stroke from the Stylize category and connect a vector level to Source, directly or through transforms. The result is the rough strokes alone; fills are not drawn. Any other kind of source passes through unchanged; see the Notes for the effects that may sit between the level and Rough Stroke.

Stroke

Drawn Width Scale multiplies the width the stroke was drawn with, and Added Width adds to it: scale 0 with an added width gives every stroke the same width. Swell In and Swell Out are the shares of a stroke over which it grows from nothing and shrinks back, like pen pressure.

Dashes

With a Dash Gap above 0 each stroke is cut into dashes Dash Length long with that gap between them, counted from the start of the stroke; Dash Offset slides the pattern along it, and animating it marches the dashes. A Dash Length of 0 draws round dots as wide as the stroke. A Dash Gap of 0 draws the stroke solid.

Width and ends

Width Variation makes the width thicken and thin smoothly at random along each stroke, by up to that share of its width, in features as long as Variation Size. Overshoot carries each copy on past both ends of the stroke, straight along its direction there, like a quick pen line; Overshoot Randomness shortens each end by a random share of that.

Copies

Copies draws each stroke that many times. The first copy follows the stroke; the others sit beside it, up to Copy Offset away, for a sketched look. The randomness amounts thin and fade copies at random.

Colour

Stroke Colour uses each stroke's own style colour, at the frame drawn when the palette is animated. Gradient runs from Start Colour to End Colour along each stroke. In both, a stroke keeps the opacity it was drawn with, including an opacity that changes along it. Over Source draws the rough strokes on top of the source drawing, which keeps its fills.

Wave and drift

Wave Amplitude bends each stroke from side to side once every Wave Length; Wave Phase slides the bends along, and Wave Phase Randomness gives each copy its own phase. Drift lets a copy wander slowly off the stroke, in features as long as Drift Size.

Reveal

Reveal Start and Reveal End pick the stretch of each stroke that is drawn, as shares of its length; animate Reveal End from 0 to 1 to draw the strokes on. Travel moves that stretch along the stroke, and Speed moves it by that share every frame without keys. An animated Speed moves the stretch by its value at each frame in turn, so slowing down to 0 stops the stretch where it is rather than sending it back. With Loop a stretch that leaves one end comes back at the other.

Timing

Hold redraws the copies, drift and randomness every that many frames, which makes the strokes boil; 0 never redraws. Seed picks the arrangement, so renders are repeatable.

Presets

The FX's presets hold Sketch, Brush and Marching Dashes.

Starting points

Sketch: Copies 3, Copy Offset 3, Drift 1.5, Hold 2. Draw on: animate Reveal End from 0 to 1. Marching dashes: Reveal End 0.2, Loop on, Speed 0.02. Brush: Drawn Width Scale 3, Swell In and Swell Out 0.4. Dotted line: Dash Length 0, Dash Gap 6. Quick ink: Width Variation 0.4, Overshoot 6, Overshoot Randomness 0.5.

Notes

Every value except Loop and Over Source can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point renders are supported. Rotating the level or scaling it evenly redraws the strokes at the new size; a stretch that scales it more one way than the other draws them at the larger scale and squeezes the result to fit.

The strokes are read from the drawing itself and drawn as a render draws them: the strokes a render hides, plain colour strokes without thickness and styles that draw no line, are left out.

Rough Stroke reads the strokes through the transforms between the level and Rough Stroke, and through what the render adds to the column: its colour filter and transparency (with Enable Column Color Filter and Transparency for Rendering on in the Scene Settings) and its Color Effect change the rough strokes as they change the drawing. The blur and haze of Multiplane Depth are not applied to the rough strokes, which stay sharp and clear.

Any other effect between the level and Rough Stroke, a Plastic deformation of the column, or a sub-xsheet in place of a level turns Rough Stroke off: its Source passes through unchanged. Put such effects after Rough Stroke instead. A deformer placed after Rough Stroke warps the image of the rough strokes; its Deform Strokes option needs the level's own strokes, so it does not apply there.

Effects that recolour a level through its palette when it renders (Colour Script, External Palette, Palette Filter) do not reach the rough strokes, which take their colours from the palette itself.
