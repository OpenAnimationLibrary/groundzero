Matte Choke

Move the edge of an image's alpha in or out. Add Matte Choke from the Matte category and connect the image to Source.

Choke / Spread is the distance in pixels at 100%: negative values move the edge in (choke), removing fringes; positive values move it out (spread), closing small gaps. Feather softens the new edge. Colours keep their hue; pixels a spread adds take the colour of the nearest pixel of the image.

The edge is where the alpha crosses one half; the feather rebuilds the antialiasing around it. Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported, and the result does not depend on render tiles.
