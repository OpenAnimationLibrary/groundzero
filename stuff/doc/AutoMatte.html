Auto Matte

Make a solid matte of a drawing's silhouette from its lines. Everything the lines enclose is filled, so a drawing with lines only, or with gaps in its paint, still gives a solid shape. Add Auto Matte from the Matte category and connect the drawing to Source; use the result as the matte of Matte In or Matte Out, or of Tone / Highlight.

Pixels at least as opaque as Line Threshold count as lines. A gap in the outline lets the outside in, as with the Fill tool: close the gap in the drawing, or lower the threshold so that faint antialiased lines count. Color is the colour of the matte, white by default.

The fill needs the whole drawing, so the Source is rendered over its bounds. A Source without bounds, such as a colour card, or one larger than 64 megapixels at the render size, has no outline to close: the matte is then its own alpha.
