Burst Lines

Draws lines that point at a centre, for focus lines, impact flashes and zooms. Add Burst Lines from the Render category; it needs no input. Lines start at Outer Radius and run towards Center; nothing is drawn inside Inner Radius. Drag the centre and the two radii in the viewer. Twist turns each line around the centre by that angle between the outer and the inner radius, and Falloff thins the lines towards the centre.

Lines

Lines is how many are drawn. Lines per Group gathers them in groups; Group Tightness pulls the lines of a group together, from evenly spread at 0 to stacked at 1, which opens gaps between groups. Position Randomness moves groups and lines off their even spacing. Length is the longest a line gets; Length Randomness shortens lines by a random share, and Start Randomness spreads where they begin.

Shape

Thickness is the widest a line gets, and Thickness Randomness thins lines by a random share. Taper narrows a line from its start to its end: 1 ends in a point. Swell In and Swell Out are the shares of a line over which it grows from nothing and shrinks back to nothing, like pen pressure.

Colour

Each line takes a colour between Colour and Second Colour; Colour Randomness is how far towards the second colour a line may go. Opacity applies to every line and Opacity Randomness fades lines by a random share.

Wave

Wave Amplitude bends each line from side to side, once every Wave Length. Wave Phase slides the bends along the lines, and Wave Phase Randomness gives each line its own phase.

Motion

Speed moves every line along itself by that distance each frame, with no keys; lines that leave one end come back at the other. Hold redraws all the lines at random every that many frames, for the flicker of lines drawn on twos or threes; 0 never redraws. Seed picks the arrangement: the same seed always gives the same lines, so renders are repeatable.

Notes

Every value except Tile can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point renders are supported. The lines are tied to the scene, not to the render tiles or the preview size. To keep lines inside a shape, use Matte In with the shape as the matte.

Starting points

Focus lines: Taper 1, Length Randomness 0.6, Outer Radius beyond the frame corners, Hold 2. Impact flash: Lines per Group 3 to 5 with Group Tightness 0.8, thick lines. Zoom: Taper 0 with Swell In and Swell Out, Start Randomness 1 and a Speed towards the centre.
